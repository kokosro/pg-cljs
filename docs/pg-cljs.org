* Introduction

=pg-cljs= is a thin, faithful ClojureScript (Node.js) wrapper of one external surface:
[[https://node-postgres.com/][node-postgres]] (the =pg= npm package, 8.23.1). Its point is
what you hand =pg= and what you get back: ClojureScript data.

This manual grows through development, alongside the surface: quick start, concepts,
surface guides (client, pool, queries, notifications, types), gotchas as observed,
testing and smoke, and the API reference of every public function. The external
surface itself (what =pg= does, with sources) is the inventory,
=docs/pg-api-inventory.org=.

Today the library exposes =pg-cljs.core/api-version= only.
